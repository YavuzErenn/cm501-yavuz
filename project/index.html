Campervans built in Bursa, made for the open road

Vanto turns everyday vans into compact motorhomes and builds light caravans you can tow with a normal car. Every vehicle is planned with you and built by hand in our workshop.

Our motto: Yola çıkma zamanı (it is time to hit the road).

What we build

  • Motorhomes

    We convert your van into a motorhome with a bed, a kitchen and a toilet, in five sizes from 8 to 17 m³. A class B licence is enough to drive it.

    Compare our motorhome sizes

  • Towed caravans

    Our caravans weigh less than 750 kg, so you can tow them without a special licence. Two lengths: 4 m and 5.3 m.

    See our caravan models
