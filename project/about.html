About Vanto

Vanto is a small workshop in Bursa that has been building campervans by hand since 2015.

Our workshop

We started with one van in a garage and now work in a 600 m² hall in the Nilüfer district. We build about 30 motorhomes and caravans a year, so every vehicle gets our full attention.

We plan each van together with you: how many people travel, how long you stay on the road and what you need on board. Then we build the furniture, the water system and the electrics in our own workshop.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a conversion take?

Between 6 and 10 weeks, depending on the size of the van and the equipment you choose.

Can you convert the van I already own?

Yes. Bring your van to the workshop and we check if it is in good condition and big enough for the layout you want.

Do I need a special driving licence?

No. All our motorhomes weigh less than 3,500 kg and all our caravans less than 750 kg, so a class B licence is enough.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes, two years on all the work we do in our workshop.

Can I visit the workshop?

Yes, during our opening hours. Call us before you come, so someone has time to show you around.
