Style guide: Vanto

Vanto is for couples and families who dream of travelling by van. The site should feel warm, handmade and ready for the road: the colours come from our Vanto 13 drawing (the orange stripe, the green hills, the dark road), and the layout stays calm so the models and dates are easy to find.

Typography

Font: system-ui (Segoe UI on Windows, San Francisco on Apple, Roboto on Android) for headings and body. Why: nothing to download, so the page is fast on mobile data at a camp site, and every system font is made to be readable on its own screens. Headings get their character from weight 700–800, not from a second font.

Headings are fluid with clamp(): they grow with the screen between a minimum and a maximum. Body text stays at 1rem so the user's browser font size is respected. Paragraphs are at most 65ch wide (--measure).

Heading 1 · --step-3 · 2rem to 3.25rem

Heading 2 · --step-2 · 1.5rem to 2.125rem

Heading 3 · --step-1 · 1.25rem to 1.5rem

Body text · --step-0 · 1rem, line height 1.5. Every Vanto is planned with you and built by hand in Bursa. A text link.

Small print · --step--1 · 0.875rem, for captions and the footer note.

Six steps. Only these values are used for margins, paddings and gaps.

Other sizes: --radius 0.5rem, --radius-pill for buttons, --border-width 0.125rem, --focus-width 0.1875rem, --target-min 1.5rem (24 px touch targets), --max-width 72rem.

Breakpoints

Mobile first: the base styles are for small screens (from 320 px). Breakpoints are in rem, so they also move when users zoom or set a larger font, and they sit where the Vanto content breaks, not at device widths.

  • Under 40rem: everything in one column; the navigation wraps below the logo; cards stack.
  • 40rem (≈ 640 px): the intro text and the motto fit next to each other.
  • 60rem (≈ 960 px): the two "What we build" cards and the drawing sit side by side; below that the drawing would be smaller than the cards.
  • Without a breakpoint: the trade fair cards use repeat(auto-fill, minmax(min(16rem, 100%), 1fr)), so they make as many columns as fit.
